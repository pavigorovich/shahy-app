Shahy Support

Shahy is a personal money tracker for iPhone: cards and cash, installments, subscriptions, debts and savings.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the help inside the app?

Settings → “How to use”. It briefly describes every section.

Do I need to sign up?

No. The app has no accounts — it works right after you install it.

How do I move my data to another iPhone?

If both iPhones use the same Apple ID and iCloud is turned on, your data appears on its own. The other way is Settings → “Create backup”, move the file to the new iPhone and choose “Restore from backup” there.

My data doesn’t appear on the second device

The iCloud section in the app’s Settings shows the sync status. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple ID and that Shahy is turned on in the iCloud section of iPhone Settings. The first sync can take a few minutes.

Which currency does the app use?

All totals are calculated in Ukrainian hryvnia (UAH). Amounts in dollars and euros can be recorded with an exchange rate to hryvnia.

How do I delete my data?

Single records are deleted right in the app, and Settings → “Delete data…” removes all transactions, installments, subscriptions or recurring payments at once. Deleting data from iCloud is described in the privacy policy.

Which devices does Shahy run on?

iPhone with iOS 27 or later.
